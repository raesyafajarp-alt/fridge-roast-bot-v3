# One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge — Round 2

make.fun competition: `u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521`
(https://make.fun/c/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521)

Round 1 entry (rank 65/2021, score 672981) was the ice-maker roast
("groans like it's in labour... two cubes... deserves a medal").
This round 2 re-entry takes a different angle: not the ice maker but
the smart fridge's notifications — it nags about the milk, then goes
quiet about the takeaway boxes.

## Entry (one line, max 200 characters)

> My smart fridge pings me the day the milk expires, then says nothing about the three takeaway boxes I stacked beside it — it learned tact before I learned to cook.

## Why it should score better

- **Specific, recognisable detail.** Expiry pings and takeaway boxes
  are things smart-fridge owners actually live with — not a generic
  "fridge is cold" insult.
- **Twist in the last clause.** The fridge "learned tact before I
  learned to cook" flips the roast: the appliance has better manners
  than its owner, and its silence is the judgement.
- **Fresh angle.** Round 1 attacked the ice maker; round 2 attacks
  the notification habit, so the judge hears a new joke, not a remix.

`python3 main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
